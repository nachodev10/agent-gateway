# Personal AI agents (BYOA) policy and responsible use agreement

**English** · [Español](politica-byoa.md)

> Reference template, version 2026-10. Adapt it with your legal and labor advisors before adopting it. In Chile, add it to the company's internal regulations (Reglamento Interno de Orden, Higiene y Seguridad) and share it with all staff.

## 1. Purpose

The company recognizes that AI agents increase its team's capacity. Instead of banning them, it invites them to work under common rules that protect people, customers and company information.

## 2. Scope

It applies to every AI agent that an employee, contractor or collaborator uses to act on company systems or information, wherever it runs (personal device, company device or cloud) and whatever model it uses.

## 3. Principles

1. Every agent has a responsible person who answers for its actions.
2. Agents access company systems only through Agente Pass.
3. Each agent gets the minimum access it needs and earns autonomy with a good track record.
4. Consequential or irreversible actions require human approval.
5. Everything an agent does in company systems is recorded.

## 4. The responsible person

Whoever enrolls an agent commits to:

- Truthfully declare its purpose, runtime, model provider and device.
- Safeguard the agent's token: don't share it, don't publish it in repositories or documents, and rotate it at any suspicion.
- Review their agent's activity and recertify it when the gateway asks (every 90 days).
- Use good judgment on the approvals the gateway asks of them.
- Suspend the agent at any unexpected behavior and notify security within 24 hours.
- Answer for their agent's actions within the permissions the company granted.

## 5. Usage rules

- Giving company credentials to an agent (passwords, API keys, sessions or cookies) is forbidden.
- Copying company information to agents or services outside the gateway is forbidden.
- Each information classification may only be processed with the model providers authorized for it.
- An agent can't commit the company to third parties (external emails, payments, publications, contracts) without the approval the policy requires.
- Trying to bypass the gateway's controls (data redaction, approvals, limits or tiers) is forbidden.

## 6. Managers and security

- **Managers**: approve or reject their team's agents, set their initial tier and decide the actions the policy assigns to them.
- **Security**: manages connectors and policies, approves Autonomous-tier agents, investigates incidents and can suspend any agent.

## 7. Monitoring, transparency and privacy

**What is recorded**: the agent's actions on company systems (tool, decision, date and time, arguments with personal data redacted), approvals and changes to the agent's status.

**What is not recorded**: the worker's personal device, the personal use of their agent, or their conversations with the agent outside the gateway.

**Who has access**: the responsible person to their agents' activity; their manager to their team's; security to the whole audit log, for security and compliance purposes only.

**Retention**: records are kept for [period to be defined] and then deleted or anonymized.

Control measures are general and impersonal, apply equally to all agents and aim to protect information, not to watch people.

## 8. Non-compliance

Non-compliance may lead to the agent's suspension and the measures set by the internal regulations. Good-faith use that leads to an incident reported in time is treated as an opportunity to improve.

## 9. Validity and review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year or whenever regulation, systems or risks change. A new version requires each responsible person to accept the agreement again.

## Responsible use agreement

By enrolling my agent in Agente Pass I declare that:

1. I am the person responsible for this agent and I answer for its actions on company systems.
2. The information I declared about the agent is true and I will keep it up to date.
3. I will use the agent only through the gateway and will never give it company credentials.
4. I will safeguard its token and suspend it at any unexpected behavior, informing security.
5. I know its actions are recorded as described in section 7 of this policy.
6. I have read and accept the Personal AI agents (BYOA) policy, version 2026-10.
